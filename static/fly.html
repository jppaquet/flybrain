Keyboard

arrow keys or WASD
↑ ←↓→ Space

Each held key drives, with spikes, neurons presynaptic to the fly's command descending neurons (found by screening their inputs). The body reads the descending neurons, one synapse downstream, and the nerve cord motor neurons they recruit; a key lights up with the firing of those descending neurons, not with the key press. The network is reset if it runs away while you drive. Space shows a looming threat to both eyes: the escape pathway fires the jump motor neuron TTMn.

The fly stands on the kart, its front legs on the steering wheel and its hind legs on the pedals, and only its motor neurons move the kart: the front legs' coxa promotors turn the wheel, the right hind leg's extensors press the accelerator, the left hind leg's flexors pull the brake. Each key drives the descending neuron that moves one leg most specifically; the kart reads that leg's motor neurons, downstream in the nerve cord. The same kart is available offline for learning (flyenv.DriveEnv).

Switchboard

keys 1–8

A number key sends a command to the fly's brain: a descending neuron that drives the front leg on that side through its own nerve cord circuit. The leg reaches toward the switch as far as its motor neurons fire in the simulation, and the switch flips only if they fire enough. The fly is tethered in front of the console, as in a lab setup.

Brain

stopped

Scale 0.5 compensates for the synaptic density of MaleCNS (at 1, activity runs away). Even so, some stimuli (wind, taste) tip the network into a self-sustained state, mostly in the central complex: the safeguard switches it off and reports it. Synaptic depression (U > 0) prevents it too, but cuts the pathways that need fast firing (TTMn, MN9).

All 165,384 neurons at their soma position, coloured by anatomical group; a neuron flashes white when it fires, and the neurons driven by a stimulus glow amber.

Direct activation (optogenetic)

Auditory neurons JO-B: a background drive that follows the volume, and a burst on every kick drum. In the connectome they reach the giant fiber, the escape descending neurons (DNp02/06/11) and the wing motor neurons: the fly spreads its wings on the beat. No path reaches the walking descending neurons.

Motor readout

Legs: motor neuron activation per muscle (empty cell: no motor neuron of that type). From the connectome: the rates of the descending and motor neurons. Hand-chosen: turning them into angles, the tripod gait and the jump trajectory.
